Prices

Simple prices, the same for Mac and PC.

  • A small job of 30 minutes or less has a fixed price.
  • Beyond that, it’s the hourly rate, with a one-hour minimum, then in 30-minute steps.
  • The diagnostic is free if you go ahead with the work.
  • Parts are billed at cost and paid upfront.
  • If the estimate changes, you are told before I go on.
  • Turnaround depends on the job, but is usually 2 business days.

By appointment, three ways

  • Drop-off

    Bring your computer at the agreed time. You get the address when you book.

  • At your home

    In the Baie-des-Chaleurs, for setup and installation at your place: printer, Wi-Fi, new computer. Time is counted from when I leave my home.

  • Remote

    Wherever you are. With your permission, I see your screen while we talk on the phone.

Mac and PC: same rates.

Before you call, note the computer’s brand and what you see on the screen. A photo of the error message helps a lot.
