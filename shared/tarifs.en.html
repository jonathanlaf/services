How time is billed

  • Remote or at the workshop

    Up to 30 minutes: the small-job rate. From 31 minutes: one hour billed.

    After the first hour, each additional half-hour started is billed. Fixed-price services keep their own rates.

  • At your home

    A minimum of one hour billed, including work on site and travel time.

    After the first hour, each additional half-hour started is billed. The small-job rate does not apply.
